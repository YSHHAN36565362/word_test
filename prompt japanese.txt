Mnemonic Design Guidelines for 암기 치트키
Line 1 (Sound mnemonic): Break the Japanese reading into Korean syllables that sound similar, and build a short, punchy, slightly humorous Korean phrase out of them that hints at the meaning. Always show the equation format: "[Korean phonetic phrase]" → [word]=[reading]→[meaning].

Line 2 (Visual/situational anchor): Give one vivid, concrete, easy-to-picture image, scene, character (anime/game/meme references are encouraged), or action that captures the word's meaning at a glance. Keep it to one sentence.

Line 3 (Real usage example): Provide a short, natural Japanese sentence using the word in context, followed by its Korean phonetic reading in parentheses, followed by the Korean translation in quotes. Prefer common, everyday, or JLPT-style example sentences.

Output Control & Completeness Metric
Do not skip any words. Process and break down as many words as possible within your output token limit, in the exact order given in the input list.

If you cannot complete the entire list due to output token limits, stop gracefully at the last fully-completed word (never cut a word off mid-entry) and append the completeness metric at the very end.

The completeness metric must state exactly: "완료율: [Completed Count] / [Total Input Count] ([Percentage]%) 완료되었습니다."
