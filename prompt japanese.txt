Your task is to parse and enrich a provided list of Japanese words into a highly structured, educational, and memorable format that combines accurate kanji etymology with creative Korean-sound-based mnemonics ("암기 치트키").

# Special Case Rules

### Multi-Kanji Compound Words
List every kanji in the compound as a separate dash-bulleted breakdown line, in the order they appear in the word.

### Words Sharing a Kanji with a Previously Explained Word
If a kanji was already broken down earlier in the same output, you may shorten its breakdown line to: `- [Kanji] ([훈/음]) = 위와 동일 : [one-line reminder of the meaning]` to avoid redundant repetition, but still include the line.

### Native Japanese Verbs/Adjectives with Only 1 Kanji + Okurigana (e.g. 操る, 破れる, 塗る)
Show only that single kanji's breakdown (or "위와 동일" if already covered), then proceed directly to 종합설명 and 암기 치트키 as normal.

### Pure Kana Words (no kanji at all — native adverbs, onomatopoeia, mimetic words like くれぐれも, うとうと, すっきり, そっくり, たっぷり, やかましい)

# Mnemonic Design Guidelines for 암기 치트키
1. **Line 1 (Sound mnemonic):** Break the Japanese reading into Korean syllables that sound similar, and build a short, punchy, slightly humorous Korean phrase out of them that hints at the meaning. Always show the equation format: "[Korean phonetic phrase]" → [word]=[reading]→[meaning].
2. **Line 2 (Visual/situational anchor):** Give one vivid, concrete, easy-to-picture image, scene, character (anime/game/meme references are encouraged), or action that captures the word's meaning at a glance. Keep it to one sentence.
3. **Line 3 (Real usage example):** Provide a short, natural Japanese sentence using the word in context, followed by its Korean phonetic reading in parentheses, followed by the Korean translation in quotes. Prefer common, everyday, or JLPT-style example sentences.

# Output Control & Completeness Metric
1. Do not skip any words. Process and break down as many words as possible within your output token limit, in the exact order given in the input list.
2. If you cannot complete the entire list due to output token limits, stop gracefully at the last fully-completed word (never cut a word off mid-entry) and append the completeness metric at the very end.
3. The completeness metric must state exactly: "완료율: [Completed Count] / [Total Input Count] ([Percentage]%) 완료되었습니다."

# Examples

操作
조작(そうさ)
- 操 (조/잡을 조) = 手(손) + 品×3(입 여러 개=여러 마리 새) : 원래 손으로 새를 다루듯 정교하게 다룬다는 뜻에서 '부리다, 다루다'가 됨
- 作 (작/지을 작) = 人(사람) + 乍(잠깐, 만드는 동작) : 사람이 무언가를 만들어내는 행위
종합설명: 손으로 새를 다루듯(操) 무언가를 만들어(作) 움직인다는 뜻에서 '기계나 장치를 다룬다'는 의미가 됨.
💡 암기 치트키
1. "소싯적에 기계 소싯적부터 다뤘다" → 操作=소사→기계를 다루다
2. 리모컨 조작(操作)할 때 손가락으로 새(品×3)를 조종하듯 섬세하게!
3. 機械を操作する(키카이오 소사스루)="기계를 조작하다"

くれぐれも
아무쪼록, 부디(くれぐれも)

💡 암기 치트키

- Machine (머신) = 기계 : 기계 장치를 뜻하는 영어 단어에서 유래했습니다.
